Movie Theater Self-Service Ticket Kiosk Requirements
This kiosk allows customers to book movie tickets through a simple self-service process.
1. View movies and showtimes: Customers can see available movies and their showtimes.
2. Choose a seat: Customers can select an available seat for their chosen showtime.
3. Purchase a ticket: Customers can purchase a ticket for their selection.
4. Receive confirmation: The system displays a confirmation after a successful purchase.
